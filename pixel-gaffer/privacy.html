Privacy policy

Pixel Gaffer '96. Last updated 1 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

What's stored on your device

Your career save, your settings and your best mini-game scores are kept on your own device, in the app's local storage, so you can carry on where you left off. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Internet use

The game downloads its own files from kinapak-ux.github.io, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. After the first time, it also works with no connection. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer email address on the game's app store page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
